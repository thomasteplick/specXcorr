Speech Pattern Parameters

Check New Speech Patterns for new speech patterns. Choose time or spectrogram plots to display. Delta Duration and Delta Pitch are in samples. The Speech Pattern chosen will be played through your audio device if fmedia is installed. Percent Voiced determines the percentage of frames in the speech pattern that are voiced; that is, have periodicities. Delta Amplitude is the fractional variation from the samples used in training.
